Everything an OPML file needs.

Collections
Every file you import stays its own collection, with its folders. Then types, then feeds, like artists, albums and songs.
Feed health
Latest item, the earliest it still lists, how often it publishes, and what the server said.
Duplicates
The same feed written two ways, http and https, or one address redirecting to another. You choose what goes.
Merge and split
Combine lists into a new one, or split one into podcasts, video and articles.
Export
Clean OPML per collection, combined, or one file per type, ready for the apps that read each.

Questions

What is an OPML file?

The list format feed readers and podcast apps use to export and import subscriptions. Look for Export OPML or Export subscriptions in your app’s settings.

How does OPMLMan decide a feed is dormant?

From the date of its newest item. By default nothing new for 30 days is slowing and for 90 days is dormant. You can change both in Settings.

Does it check feeds in the background?

No. Feeds are only checked when you tap Check.

Does it play podcasts or show articles?

No. OPMLMan manages and checks your lists. For podcasts, export a clean list and import it into Opiuml, from the same developer.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s free, with no ads and no tracking. The optional tip jar unlocks nothing.
